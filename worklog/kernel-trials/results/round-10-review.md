# Round 10 — independent review (verbatim)

Seat: a Sonnet subagent (the plan's author wrote on Fable); read-only; 2026-10-13, before any
live call. Verdict: run after amendments (not as written). The amendments taken are listed in the
plan's `## Review`.

1. Hypothesis, Axis, Decision rule (lines 9-20, 24-25, 53-57): the criterion cannot be met as stated. The runner's p is the peak of the answer distribution (`peak(leaf)` in oracle-test.mjs), i.e. confidence in the decided side, so with two options it is always at least 0.5. The three runs happen at the declared 0.5, and the sweep only re-labels cells whose p falls below 0.6 or 0.7 as `unknown`. Line 38 counts `unknown` as agreeing with neither, so agreement can only fall or stay equal as the threshold rises. "Improves by at least 2 cells" (line 55) is therefore impossible, and the first refuting cell (line 16) fires by construction. Amendment: state p's semantics and which side(s) the threshold demotes (4c's notes suggest the shipped check reads `holds` at 0.51 as a miss, so say whether demotion is symmetric). Then replace agreement with measures a threshold can move: wrong-side decided cells (lead says fails, judge says holds at p>=t, and the reverse) as the cost, unknown cells as coverage, and the document verdict each unknown produces under the constraint. The rule must say what `unknown` does to a `require` (NO-GO, a finding, or pass).

2. Hypothesis, Material (lines 9-11, 28-34): the plan says goals 01-04 and designs 02-05, but the label files on disk cover only goals 01-03 (19 cells) and designs 02, 03, 05 (30 rows). Goal 04 has no label file. Design 04 is labelled only as the 4c `good` fixture. Design 06 is untracked and carries a witness table (line 91 of its file) the plan does not mention. Amendment: say which documents are in or out and the exact cell count per oracle, and decide on design 06 (a draft that may still be edited mid-round, so exclude it or freeze it). Give the draft status of every document used.

3. Material, Hypothesis (lines 28-34): "re-derived blind" is not blind. The lead has seen the judge's p on these exact cells in rounds 4, 4c and 6. Five cells were rewritten after the judge flagged them (design/02 x2, design/05 x2, goals/01 R5), so those labels were shaped by the judge. Overwriting `labels.yaml` and `witness-labels.yaml` (lines 62-63) also destroys the 4c baseline the plan compares to. Amendment: write the new labels to new files (for example `labels-r10.yaml`), commit them before the run, and mark each cell as unchanged since the round it was labelled in, or rewritten after judge exposure (reported separately). Replace "blind" with "pre-registered, judge-exposed" where that is true.

4. Material, Measurements (lines 28-41): about 13 of the 19 goal cells are the goals/02-03 "Evidence required" controls, all labelled `fails`, medium confidence. The shipped constraint never asks them; they run through a fixture-local rule. They are easy, and only goals/01's 6 cells (plus goal 04's) are the real middling population. A 2-cell margin on 6 cells, and deciles of p over 6 to 19 cells (line 39), carry no information. Amendment: report goal-oracle results on the evidence-bearing cells only, with the controls reported separately. Drop deciles for n below about 20 and print the sorted p values. Say plainly that the goal-oracle decision has too few cells to raise a threshold and will default to 0.5 unless something else is measured, or add cells (goal 04, other repositories' goals).

5. Axis, Predicate (lines 24-25, 62-65): the real-cell runs use `--repeat 2` and the seeded runs have no `--repeat`, but the plan never says how two repeats become one p per cell: the mean, the min, or a cell counted as unstable when the repeats disagree. Round 4c recorded a flip at the threshold (gross row 13, 0.51 then `fails`). Amendment: fix the aggregation (suggest min of per-repeat peak, and report flips separately). Define the boundary (>= or >) so a p of exactly 0.6 is not ambiguous. Run the seeded sets with `--repeat 2` as well, or state why not.

6. Material, Predicate (lines 34, 64-65): the seeded fixtures were measured under the batched shape in rounds 4 and 4c. The plan re-runs them once per-atom, but the "keeps its side" test compares against which baseline? The 4c ledger numbers (for example row 13 at 0.51) are batched-shape. Amendment: say that the per-atom run replaces the baseline, and tabulate the old batched p beside the new per-atom p per fixture cell, so the reader can see the shape change apart from the threshold change. Pass `--request-shape per-atom` explicitly on all four commands (both shipped modules declare per-atom, but the runner's default and the fixture-local `module.yaml` for witnesses should be confirmed).

7. Expected outcomes (lines 45-50): two of four rows have no refuting cell ("best threshold" and "share in [0.5,0.7)", the "—" and blank cells), against the method's rule that every expectation gets one. The 0.6-for-evidence prediction cites "the goals/01 band", but that band is p at 0.55-0.66 on a handful of cells, so by line 55 the cell count cannot clear the +2 bar anyway. Line 49 also contradicts itself: "0 change side" yet names the thinnest seeded cell as "a known miss". Raising to 0.6 would move gross row 13 from `holds` 0.51 to `unknown`, which is a side change. Amendment: add a refuting cell to each row. Per oracle, state expectation and refuter separately (the oracles are conflated in "both oracles"). Define "side" as one of holds/fails/unknown and say whether a seeded miss moving to `unknown` is an improvement, a neutral change or a loss.

8. Hypothesis, third refuting cell vs. expectations (lines 18-20 and 46): "disagree on more than 30% of real cells at every threshold" and "agreement at 0.5 below 70%" are the same condition stated twice with different wording. The 30% rule is also the only one that distinguishes "question is wrong" from "threshold is wrong", but nothing in the decision table says what to record in that case. Amendment: merge them, and add a row to the decision table.

9. Decision rule (lines 53-57): it is prose, not a table, and omits cases: two thresholds tie; the best threshold differs by oracle; a seeded cell moves only on one repeat; a genuine seeded cell drops to `unknown`; a seeded defective cell at 0.51 rises to `unknown` (arguably good); and no run of the real track is `complete`. Amendment: provide a table of outcome, action, and where it is recorded. "Calibration note on the oracle" needs a named location (the oracle's comment block in `doc-verify/modules/design.yaml` or `doc-verify/lib/goal.yaml`). Also say that a raised threshold must be applied through the same one-line mechanism the other oracle thresholds use and which tests change.

10. Predicate, builder's deliverable (lines 61-71): `threshold-sweep.mjs` does not exist yet (confirmed: not under probe/), and its "seeded self-test on a synthetic sidecar" is not specified. Its input format is `oracle-fixtures/.leaves-<ledger>.json`, which is hidden and written by promotion runs. Under seeded-defect-gate, say what defect it must flip: a sidecar where one cell has a p of exactly the boundary, one cell with a repeat disagreement, one with a missing repeat, one cell unlabelled. Say what "complete false" prints and the exit code. Also state that the sweep must not call the judge, since the axis claim is "no new judge calls".

11. Predicate, cost (lines 62-65): no question count is projected. The runner supports `--cap` and a mock projection, but the plan gives only caps (120/150/120/150). My own estimate under per-atom with `--no-spans`: goal real about 19 x 2 = 38 questions; design real 30 x 2 = 60; evidence seeded about 55; witness seeded 6 fixtures x 14 rows = 84. The totals sit under the caps, but the plan should include the mock projection and a total, as round 4c's 394 + 109 did. It should also state whether the run is mock-first, and what a cap trip means (incomplete, not a verdict).

12. Predicate (line 64): `--invariant-base c632a91` appears only on the evidence seeded command and not on the witness seeded command. Say why, and confirm the commit still exists and the fixture tables are unchanged since it (the goal fixtures were not touched in rounds 4c to 9 except via real-cells; confirm). Also the seeded fixture set for each oracle should be named by file (`oracle-fixtures/evidence_reasons/*.md`, `witness_observable_in_row/fixtures.yaml`), since "11 fixtures" and "6 fixtures" are counts, and `defective-wrongrow.md`, which is a known miss (the judge never sees the requirement text), would sit in the count with no stated expected side.

13. Left unmeasured (lines 73-76): incomplete. Missing: the wrong-row and swapped-witness gap (ledger item 4 and the 4c verdict), which a threshold cannot touch but which sits in the same item and may be read as closed if 0.5 stays; the four `module-*` libraries pinned to `batched` (whose thresholds this does not set); per-run judge variance beyond two repeats; goal-04 or design-06 as held-out cells; and that seeded cells in [0.5, 0.6) are the real test but there are almost none. Add these so the verdict cannot be over-read.

14. Review section and status (lines 3, 78-80): the review file is named `results/round-10-review.md` and does not exist yet, which is expected, but the plan says nothing of how findings are dispositioned. Round 9 recorded each finding taken, deferred or rejected. Add the same table.

Verdict: do not run as written, run after amendments. The decision rule rests on a metric (agreement with `unknown` counted against it) that cannot improve when a threshold rises (finding 1). The label files also do not match the stated material (finding 2), and the sweep tool does not exist (finding 10).
